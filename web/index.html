01 / What the factors remove.

Training-only correlation PCA

Means, sample scales, eigenvectors and AR(1) coefficients use training days only. They remain fixed for every held-out day. Retaining more factors removes more training variation, not necessarily more risk.

Each bar is its share of the six-asset correlation trace. Retained components are teal. Eigenvector signs are arbitrary; the projector and residuals are sign-invariant.

03 / Open the residual basket.

Frozen raw-return coefficients, not trade sizes

For the selected asset i, the diagnostic basket is wⱼ = δᵢⱼ - σᵢPᵢⱼ/σⱼ. Its centered return equals that asset's PCA residual. These are unnormalized coefficients, not dollar allocations. Factor exposure is zero only in the retained, fitted PC geometry.

No dollar neutrality or out-of-sample economic beta neutrality is promised. The mean offset is separate: residual = Σwⱼrⱼ - Σwⱼμⱼ. No hedge execution, gross scaling, borrow constraint or transaction-cost model is applied.

A fitted bridge.

Training residuals are demeaned, so their cumulative path starts and ends near zero by construction. This fitted bridge can favor apparent reversion. AR(1) eligibility is a parameter-domain check, not proof of stationarity, cointegration or profitability.

A narrow OU mapping.

Fit Xₜ₊₁ = a + bXₜ + e on training transitions. Only 0 < b < 1 with positive innovation variance maps to the continuous-time mean-reverting OU model used here. Other fits stay flat. No coefficient clipping or fallback fit is used.

A frozen experiment.

This is not the rolling empirical strategy in Avellaneda and Lee. No calibration, data cleaning, costs, risk controls or live deployment are demonstrated. Changing the seed or factor count is not a search for a profitable setting. Native Safari/Android untested.
